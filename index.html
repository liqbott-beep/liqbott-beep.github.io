Day Counter

Stick-figure survival stories, one day at a time.

Day Counter makes animated "could you survive?" stories. Each video follows a stick-figure hero through a survival scenario day by day: a blackout that never ends, a year in a bunker, a winter alone in a cabin.

  • Full videos every Tuesday and Friday on YouTube
  • Short clips on Instagram and TikTok

About the "liqchannel" app

liqchannel is the private tool the owner of Day Counter uses to publish the channel's own content. It signs in only to the owner's own accounts:

  • YouTube: uploads and schedules videos and reads the channel's analytics.
  • TikTok: posts short clips of each video to the owner's TikTok account (Content Posting API) and reads those clips' view counts.
  • Instagram: posts the same clips as Reels to the owner's professional account and reads their insights.

It is not offered to other users and nobody else can sign in to it.
